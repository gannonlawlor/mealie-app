# Cookbook - Mealie Companion privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026.

Cookbook is an independent client for saving recipes locally and connecting to a
Mealie server you choose. It does not operate a hosted recipe account service.

## Information handled by the app

- Recipes, photos, favorites, shopping items, settings, and pending imports are
  stored on your device as needed for the features you use.
- If you connect to Mealie, the app sends your sign-in details to that server and
  exchanges recipes, meal plans, shopping lists, and account information with it.
  Session credentials are retained on your device so you can stay signed in.
  The server operator controls that server's storage, logs, backups, and policies.
- Importing a recipe contacts the recipe website and image hosts, either directly
  from your device in local mode or through your Mealie server in server mode.
  Those services receive normal network request information, such as your IP
  address. Their own privacy policies apply.
- On iOS, optional iCloud sync sends local recipe data to your Apple iCloud
  account. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply. Sync availability depends on
  your iCloud settings and connection.
- On iOS, optional Reminders access lets the app find a grocery list and add
  ingredients to it. Access is requested when you enable the feature. You can
  revoke access in system settings.

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking identifiers,
or a developer-operated telemetry endpoint. App stores and operating systems may
separately collect diagnostic or distribution information under their own settings
and policies. If you submit feedback or a public issue, the information you choose
to include is handled by that service.

## Retention, deletion, and choices

You can delete recipes and shopping items using the app. Signing out removes the
active server credentials from the app; it does not delete your Mealie account or
server data. Manage server account deletion, server backups, and retention with
your Mealie administrator. Local recipes are preserved when switching modes.

Device data remains until you delete it or remove the app, subject to operating
system backups and any iCloud copies. Removing the app does not necessarily remove
cloud or server copies, or credentials retained by the operating system's secure
storage. Sign out before removing the app to clear its active session. Manage
backups and cloud storage through your device and Apple account settings. Disable
optional integrations in the app or system settings.

Use HTTPS for server connections. If you choose HTTP, network traffic, including
sign-in details, is not encrypted in transit. Use it only on a network you trust.

## Support and policy changes

Questions about the app or this policy can be raised through the
[project issue tracker](https://github.com/gannonlawlor/mealie-app/issues).
Issues are public: do not post passwords, tokens, personal recipes, or private
server addresses. For your server's policies or deletion requests, contact its
administrator. Future policy changes will be published here with an updated date.
